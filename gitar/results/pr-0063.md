Insights screen adds room fill bars, fair seating timing, and per-talk engagement curves, with the slot defaulting to the newest numbers. Consider basing the engagement history window on the slot's newest sample instead of `now()`, since after two hours the history goes empty and the x-axis keeps stretching once sampling stops.

With no `?slot=`, the route now picks the slot with the newest sample, even after sampling for that slot has stopped. But `history` is still limited to `at > now() - 120 min`, while `latest` has no time limit. On the client, `span` runs from `startedAt` to `generatedAt` (that is, now).

Here is what happens once a slot's sampling ends, for example after the last talk or when replaying rehearsal data:
- The x-axis keeps growing on every 5 s refresh, so the curves bunch up against the left edge.
- After two hours, `history` comes back empty. `engagement` becomes `null` and `startedAt` falls back to `now`. The screen then says "No talk has started yet in this slot", while the room bars and totals still show that slot's seat counts.

Fix: base the history window on the slot's newest sample, not on `now()`, and return that last sample time as the chart's end point for `span`.

<details>
<summary>Anchor the history window to the slot's last sample and expose it for the chart span.</summary>

````

    "SELECT room, at, taken FROM occupancy_samples WHERE slot = %s"
    " AND at > %s - make_interval(mins => %s) ORDER BY at", (slot, last, HISTORY_MINUTES)
````
